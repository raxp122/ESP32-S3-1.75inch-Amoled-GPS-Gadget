AmuletOS

Firmware __VERSION__ · Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75

A pendant for photos, GIFs and videos on the round AMOLED screen. This page installs the firmware over USB the first time; after that the pendant updates itself over Wi-Fi.

1 · Install

Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Connect the board with a USB data cable, press the button and pick the board's serial port ("USB JTAG/serial debug unit").

This browser cannot talk to USB devices: use desktop Chrome or Edge. This page needs HTTPS.

Updating an AmuletOS board? Leave Erase device unchecked: settings and Wi-Fi are kept. Photos and videos are on the microSD and are never touched.

The board is not found? Try another cable (many only charge). If it still does not show up, hold BOOT, press and release PWR (or plug the cable in), release BOOT.

2 · First start

  1. Insert a microSD card formatted FAT32.
  2. On the pendant: Settings › Wi-Fi › From your phone, scan the QR code and pick your network.
  3. Open Upload and scan its QR code with the phone: choose photos, GIFs or videos, crop them to the circle and send them.
  4. Press BOOT on the home screen to wear it: pendant mode.
